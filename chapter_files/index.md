These are the course notes (moving towards a book) focusing on teaching mathematically grounded data science to upper-division undergraduates and graduate students.  They are generated downstream of the following texts: 

* Introduction to Probabilistic Machine Learning, by Kevin Murphy
* Data Driven Science and Engineering: Machine Learning, Dynamical Systems, and Control, by Steven L. Brunton, J. Nathan Kutz
* Machine Learning: A Bayesian and Optimization Perspective, by Sergios Theodoridis
* Machine Learning with PyTorch and Scikit-Learn, by Sebastian Raschka, Yuxi Liu, and Vahid Mirjalili
* All of Statistics: A Concise Course in Statistical Inference, by Larry Wasserman 
* Deep Learning Architectures: A Mathematical Approach, by Ovidiu Calin
